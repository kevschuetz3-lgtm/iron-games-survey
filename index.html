Help us build Colorado's Fitness Festival

For six years, the Koda Iron Games has been one of Colorado's biggest CrossFit competitions. For year 7 in 2027, we want to make it something much bigger: a huge Colorado Fitness Festival - more events, more divisions, more ways to compete, and a weekend the whole fitness community circles on the calendar.

Before we plan 2027, we want to hear from you. Whether you've competed every year or haven't competed yet, your answers decide what changes next.
